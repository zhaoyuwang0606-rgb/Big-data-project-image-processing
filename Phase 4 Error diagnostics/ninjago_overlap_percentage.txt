=== Identity Overlap: NINJAGO vs The LEGO NINJAGO Movie ===

Total minifigures in 'NINJAGO': 944
Total minifigures in 'The LEGO NINJAGO Movie': 90

Of the 90 minifigures in the Movie category:
 - 33 (36.7%) are variants of characters that ALSO exist in the regular NINJAGO TV series.
 - Only 57 (63.3%) are truly exclusive to the movie.

List of shared characters found across both:
  - Cole
  - Dareth
  - Garmadon
  - Jay Walker
  - Kai
  - Lil' Nelson
  - Lloyd Garmadon
  - Misako
  - Mystake
  - Nya
  - Ray
  - Wu
  - Zane

Conclusion:
Nearly half of the 'The LEGO NINJAGO Movie' category consists of characters that the model has already learned to associate with the 'NINJAGO' category. Penalizing the model for classifying a Movie-Lloyd as a Regular-Lloyd is a semantic artifact, not a visual failure.
